excerpt: 'This paper attempts to explore the impact of broad money supply on the growth of the economy of Bangladesh spanning from 1976 to 2019. The study has employed autoregressive distributed lag (ARDL) modelling approach to examine both short-run and long-run link between the variables. The model used in the study is specified by four macroeconomic variables: Per Capita GDP growth rate, broad money supply growth rate, GDP deflator and official exchange rate. Results from the study indicate that though there exists a long-run relationship between the growth of the economy and the supply of money, the impact of the supply of money is statistically not significant on the growth of the economy. Thus, the findings suggest the money market of Bangladesh needs overhauling effectively to stimulate economic growth.'
